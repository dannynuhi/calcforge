# Revenue Roadmap

CalcForge can be prepared for display-ad monetization, but earnings are not guaranteed. Results depend on Google indexing, rankings, traffic, approval, ad demand, page quality, and time.

## Phase 1: Site Online And Verified

- Keep https://calcforge-nine.vercel.app live.
- Keep the Google verification file in `public/`.
- Confirm Search Console ownership.

## Phase 2: Sitemap Submitted

- Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
- Confirm `robots.txt` and `image-sitemap.xml` are accessible.

## Phase 3: Indexing And Impressions

- Wait for Google to crawl pages.
- Watch impressions, indexed pages, and crawl issues.
- Improve pages that get impressions but low clicks.

## Phase 4: Content Expansion

- Add small batches of genuinely useful calculators and guides.
- Avoid doorway pages, duplicate pages, and filler.
- Keep every page useful without ads.

## Phase 5: AdSense Application

- Apply only after the site has enough useful original content and Search Console is working.
- Use the official site URL: https://calcforge-nine.vercel.app

## Phase 6: Auto Ads

- Add `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT` only after approval.
- Enable Auto ads if desired.
- Keep ad density modest.

## Phase 7: Optimization After Traffic Appears

- Review Search Console and AdSense data.
- Improve internal links, page clarity, and ad placement only after there is enough traffic to learn from.

Realistic timeline: weeks to months, not instant.
