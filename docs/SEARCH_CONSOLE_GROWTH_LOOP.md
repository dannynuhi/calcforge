# Search Console Growth Loop

Official site URL:

https://calcforge-nine.vercel.app

This is a low-maintenance monthly process for improving CalcForge without chasing page count or making broad changes too quickly.

## Monthly Process

1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Review pages with impressions.
3. Find pages with impressions but low clicks.
4. Improve the title and meta description for one or two of those pages if the search intent is clear.
5. Review queries where a page appears but does not fully answer the searcher need.
6. Add a better example, clearer formula explanation, common mistake, or short FAQ only when it helps the visible page.
7. Add one or two natural internal links from related calculators or guides.
8. Avoid changing many pages at once; small changes are easier to evaluate.
9. Redeploy.
10. Wait a few weeks, then re-check the same pages.

## What To Prioritize

- Pages that already get impressions.
- Finance, debt, loan, percentage, unit comparison, date/time, and practical planning pages.
- Pages where the calculator is useful but the explanation could be clearer.
- Pages with strong intent but weak click-through rate.

## What To Avoid

- Adding many near-duplicate pages.
- Creating pages only for search engines.
- Making promises about rankings, approval, traffic, or revenue.
- Publishing unsupported authority claims.
- Enabling real ads before the site is ready and approved.
